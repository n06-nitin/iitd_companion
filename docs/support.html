Common questions

Why do I still have to type a CAPTCHA?

Moodle and RollCall show a CAPTCHA on their login pages. The app fills in your Kerberos ID and password, and you type only the CAPTCHA. Webmail and Moodle New have no CAPTCHA, so they sign you in by themselves.

It says “Sign-in failed · check password”

The portal turned down your saved login. If you changed your Kerberos password, open the ⋮ menu, choose Edit saved login, and save the new one. Then tap the reload button to try again. After several failed attempts Webmail may ask for an extra check; complete it on the page and sign in once.

A portal won't load

Tap the reload button. If it still fails, check your internet connection, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Open in browser to see whether the IIT Delhi site itself is down.

How do I go back a page, or back to a portal's home?

On Android, use the back button. On iPhone, swipe in from the left edge of the page. Tapping the tab that's already open takes that portal back to its home page.

RollCall's QR scanner won't open the camera

Allow camera access for IITD Companion: on Android in Settings › Apps › IITD Companion › Permissions, on iPhone in Settings › IITD Companion › Camera. Then reload the RollCall tab.

Where do downloaded files go?

On Android, to your Downloads folder. On iPhone, to the Files app, under On My iPhone › IITD Companion › Downloads.

How do I remove my login?

Open the ⋮ menu and choose Forget login & sign out. This deletes your saved Kerberos ID and password and signs you out of every portal. Uninstalling the app removes everything too.

Is this an official IIT Delhi app?

No. IITD Companion is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IIT Delhi. For problems with the portals themselves, such as your Kerberos account or a course page, contact IIT Delhi's Computer Services Centre.
